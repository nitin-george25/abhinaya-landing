Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Food and beverages at Abhinaya Cinemas are sold by CAFE ABHINAYA (GSTIN 32ACWPG5892L1ZV), at the cinema at M C Road, Perunna, Changanacherry — Kottayam, Kerala 686101.

This policy covers food and beverage orders placed at our self-service kiosks and F&B counter. Cinema tickets are covered separately by our Terms of Use.

1. Cancelling an order

Food and beverage items are prepared for collection as soon as payment is confirmed. Because preparation begins immediately, an order cannot normally be cancelled once payment has been completed. Before you complete payment you may abandon the order at any point on the kiosk — nothing is charged until your UPI application confirms the payment.

2. When we will refund you

We will refund an order in full in any of these cases:

  • An item you paid for is unavailable and cannot be supplied.
  • You were served the wrong item, or an item not fit to consume.
  • Payment was debited but no order token or receipt was produced.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.

3. Failed or duplicate payments

If money left your account but the kiosk issued no order token, the transaction did not complete at our end. Such amounts are ordinarily reversed automatically by your bank or UPI provider within 5–7 working days. If it has not reached you by then, contact us with the UPI reference number and we will trace it.

4. How to raise a request

  • At the cinema. Take your token or receipt to the F&B counter the same day. This is the fastest route and most issues are settled on the spot.
  • Afterwards. Email hello@abhinayacinemas.com or call +91 481 406 6453 with your order number, the date, the amount and the UPI reference. Please contact us within 7 days of the transaction.

5. How refunds are paid

  • Always to the original payment method. We do not refund to a different account, and we do not pay cash against a UPI payment.
  • Requests at the counter are decided immediately. Requests by email are decided within 2 working days.
  • Approved refunds are initiated within 2 working days and typically credited within 5–7 working days. Final timing is controlled by your bank or UPI provider, not by us.
  • No deduction is made. You receive the full amount paid.

Need help with an order? Write to hello@abhinayacinemas.com or call +91 481 406 6453.
