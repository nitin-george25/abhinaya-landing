Pricing

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Food and beverages at Abhinaya Cinemas are sold by CAFE ABHINAYA (GSTIN 32ACWPG5892L1ZV), at the cinema at M C Road, Perunna, Changanacherry — Kottayam, Kerala 686101.

1. What you see is what you pay

  • All prices are shown in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • Every price displayed on the kiosk is inclusive of GST. The tax is already in the number on the screen.
  • There is no convenience fee, no service charge, no packaging charge and no payment-gateway fee. The amount shown beside the QR code is the amount you pay.
  • You are charged the price displayed at the moment you place the order, even if it changes afterwards.

2. Range of prices

Food and beverage items ordinarily range from ₹20 to ₹500 per item. The kiosk shows the current price of every item before you add it, and the full total before you pay.

3. Tax and invoicing

  • GST is charged at the rate applicable to each item under Indian GST law.
  • A GST tax invoice is printed for every order, showing our GSTIN, the invoice number and date, the CGST and SGST components, and the total paid.
  • GSTIN 32ACWPG5892L1ZV. Place of supply: Kerala (32).

4. Changes to prices

Prices may be revised at any time without prior notice, for example when supplier costs or tax rates change. Revised prices apply only to orders placed after the change, never retrospectively to an order already paid for.

5. How you can pay

Payment at the kiosk is by UPI. You scan the QR code on the screen with any UPI application and approve the payment on your own phone. We never see, ask for, or store your UPI PIN, card number or banking credentials.
