Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Food and beverages at Abhinaya Cinemas are sold by CAFE ABHINAYA (GSTIN 32ACWPG5892L1ZV), at the cinema at M C Road, Perunna, Changanacherry — Kottayam, Kerala 686101.

We do not ship anything. Food and beverages are sold for collection in person at our cinema in Changanacherry. There is no courier, no dispatch, and no delivery to any address.

1. How your order reaches you

You order and pay at a self-service kiosk or at the F&B counter inside the cinema. The kiosk issues an order token. Your order is prepared at our counter and handed to you when you present that token.

  • Where. The F&B counter at Abhinaya Cinemas, M C Road, Perunna, Changanacherry — Kottayam, Kerala 686101. Orders cannot be collected anywhere else.
  • When. Orders are normally ready within 5–10 minutes of payment. Immediately before a show, or during an interval, this may take longer.
  • Cost. There is no delivery charge, because there is no delivery.

2. Availability

Ordering is available only while the cinema is open and the F&B counter is operating, which follows the day's show times. Items are subject to availability, and the kiosk will not let you order something that has sold out.

3. Uncollected orders

Please collect your order on the day it is placed. Prepared food not collected before the counter closes cannot be held and is treated as served. If you could not collect for a reason within our control, see our Cancellation & Refunds policy.
